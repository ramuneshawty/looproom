# Third-party software

The bundled video engine is FFmpeg 7.1, obtained from the Windows x86-64 wheel of imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 on PyPI.

- FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/
- FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz
- Imageio-ffmpeg distribution and build information: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg/tree/v0.6.0
- Distribution: https://pypi.org/project/imageio-ffmpeg/0.6.0/

This copy is for your personal local use. FFmpeg is separate software with its own license; consult the accompanying FFmpeg license notice and the binary's `-L` output before redistributing a packaged copy.
